Guitar Tuner Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Guitar Tuner does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

  • The app uses the microphone only while it is open, to hear the pitch of the string you play. Audio is analyzed on the device as it arrives and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party services.
  • The app makes no network requests while you use it.

Guitar Tuner is open source, so you can check this yourself: github.com/iCodeForBananas/guitar-tuner. Questions can be raised as an issue there.
